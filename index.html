Paramount+ · Technical Producer, Creative Content · 2022–2024

Three production tools consolidated into Silverstack Lab.

An audit found assets losing chain of custody at handoffs. I built the business case, secured leadership approval, and consolidated processing into Silverstack Lab. That change reduced daily processing from six hours to 30–45 minutes.

The Silverstack Lab workflow then triggered updates into the Airtable database I built. Airtable became the department-wide reference for personnel, schedules, payroll, and production reporting across a $3M annual portfolio.

Across the production workflow, error margin dropped to effectively zero and throughput tripled, with no added headcount.
